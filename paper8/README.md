# Paper 8 reproduction package

*A Wear-Activated Density-Release Refinement of the Hermes Gravity Equation: A Paper 1 Addendum*
(McGinty 2026). Zenodo, all versions: 10.5281/zenodo.20699990.

One command rebuilds the paper's SPARC and M33 numbers from public inputs and checks
them. Candidates 1 and 2 are not rebuilt (see below), and the M33 numbers are checked
on Paper 7's gate as corrected in Paper 7 v3, not as versions 1 and 2 printed them
(see Version notes):

```bash
python paper8/reproduce.py --sparc /path/to/rotmod_dir      # or set SPARC_DIR
```

The SPARC rotation curves (Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert 2016) are not redistributed here.
The repository README shows how to fetch them from Zenodo 16284118; CI does the same and
runs this script on every push. The script needs NumPy and SciPy (`requirements.txt`).
It prints one line per check with the place in version 3 of the paper that reports the
number ("Table 5.1" is the table in Section 5.1), writes `paper8/results/`, and exits 1
if any check fails; "(support)" marks a check behind a statement whose number is not printed. Until version 3 is deposited, many checked numbers have no
counterpart in the version 2 texts shipped here (see Version notes).

## What it checks

| Group | What |
|---|---|
| Table 5.1 | medians, counts above 5 and 10, casualties, improvements, wins against MOND, per-galaxy and pooled median velocity residuals |
| Gate convention | the same comparison on Paper 1's chain-rule gate (`paper9/hermes_clusters/gate.py`) |
| Stellar M/L | baseline, refinement and MOND at 0.5 for disks and 0.7 for bulges, with g98 recomputed |
| Candidates and constants | candidates 3 to 5, other closed-form constants that also give zero casualties, and the operator with S, Gamma or the pi cap removed |
| Supplements B and C | the outer-five residual audit and the improved-galaxy comparison |
| M33 | baseline, refinement and component-age sweep on Paper 7's board, with its gate as corrected in Paper 7 v3, plus MOND on the same board |
| Shipped data | SHA-256 of `expected/`; the recomputed per-galaxy, per-radius, audit and summary tables against the shipped CSV files in `expected/` |

Candidates 1 and 2 of the paper's Section 3 are not rebuilt: their definitions are not
specific enough to rerun, and their table cells come from the original runs.

## Conventions

Each of these changes a checked value if altered.

- **Gate:** the log-grid gate in `paper1/hermes_gate_phi.py`. The chain-rule gate that
  reproduces Paper 1's published scores gives a refinement median of 1.150, not 1.189
  (check R.2; the two gates are described in `docs/gate_version_history.md`).
- **Velocity:** V = sqrt(max(g_model, 0) R), as in Paper 1. g_model < 0 only where
  g_bar < 0, at two points of UGC 01281.
- **MOND:** simple interpolating function, a0 = 1.2e-10 m/s^2 with the exact parsec,
  3702.813 (km/s)^2/kpc.
- **chi2/N:** the mean over points of (Vobs - V)^2 / (errV^2 + 386).
- **Outer five:** the last five radii of each rotmod file, with residual = V_model - V_obs.
- **M33:**
  - g98 is the 98th percentile of the board's g_bar.
  - The inner disk (R <= 10 kpc) uses the global age (6.0 Gyr, or 7.1 Gyr for the conservative bracket), and the outer disk uses the outer age.
  - W always uses the global age.

## Files

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `verify_eta.py` | The operator: `eta_WA(R)`, the refined `g_model(R)`, the Paper 1 baseline and MOND |
| `reproduce.py` | Rebuilds and checks the paper's numbers |
| `expected/` | The paper's ten production CSV files, public for the first time here (the Zenodo records of versions 1 and 2 hold only the PDFs); see below |
| `MANIFEST_SHA256.txt` | SHA-256 of every file in this folder |
| `paper8_main.md`, `paper8_supplementary_materials.md` | The version 2 paper texts, the sources of the version 2 PDFs on Zenodo (each version's text is committed after its Zenodo deposit) |

`expected/`:

- **Seven SPARC tables:** the production files of May 2026, unchanged, including their two galaxy-name spellings (`IC4202`, `NGC0100`).
  - `paper8_sparc_133_model_comparison.csv`: per galaxy, 133 rows.
  - `paper8_sparc_per_radius_baseline_mond_wear_eta.csv`: per radius, 3,073 rows.
  - `paper8_sparc_aggregate_metrics.csv`: the three models.
  - `paper8_iteration_comparison_metrics.csv`: baseline, MOND, candidates 3 to 5 (candidate 5 at logistic width 1/pi) and the final operator.
  - `paper8_sparc_outer_signed_residual_audit_last3_last5.csv`: the outer-tail audit.
  - `paper8_sparc_13_improved_galaxies_characterization.csv`: the 13 improved galaxies.
  - `paper8_sparc_outer5_sign_flip_watchlist.csv`: the 16 sign-flip galaxies.
- **Three M33 tables** (`paper8_m33_wear_eta_component_age_summary.csv`, `..._summary_long.csv`, `..._radial_profile.csv`): regenerated by `reproduce.py` on the gate as corrected in Paper 7 v3.

## Version notes

Versions 1 and 2 of the paper printed every M33 number (the abstract, Section 1, the M33
column of the Section 3 table, Section 5.2, and Supplements A and D) from Paper 7's
superseded gate, for example:
- outer disk 5.79, η alone 2.50, component age alone 4.05;
- η plus 2 or 1 Gyr: 1.44 and 1.21;
- inner disk 0.80, outer share 86%.

On the corrected gate these become 5.86, 2.60, 4.12, 1.53, 1.30, 0.93 and 85%, which is what the checks here use. Every cell of Supplement D's two sweep tables changes too (for example 1.571 to 1.682 for the full board at a uniform 6.0 Gyr), as do the M33 cells of candidates 3 and 5 (2.50 to 2.59, and 1.02 to 1.10 at 2 Gyr). The M33 cells of candidates 1 and 2 are not recomputed.

Version 2's recomputation note gave a precision of "better than 3 x 10^-13". The rebuild agrees with the per-galaxy scores to 9.3e-13 (log-grid, check F.0) and 3.6e-13 (chain-rule, check R.0), so those two checks use 1e-11 and the table comparisons 1e-9.

Version 3 of the paper is being prepared from this package. It adds the stellar-M/L, chain-rule and alternative-constant results and the corrected M33 values. Its texts replace `paper8_main.md` and `paper8_supplementary_materials.md` once it is deposited.
